Privacy Policy — Least Privilege Press Publisher App

Last updated: 16 October 2026

The Least Privilege Press Publisher app ("the App") is a self-hosted tool operated by

Least Privilege Press ("we", "us"). It is used by the business owner only, to publish

pins to the business's own Pinterest account through the official Pinterest API.

What the App accesses

With the account owner's authorization (Pinterest OAuth), the App can read the owner's

boards and pins and create pins and boards on the owner's own account. The App is not

offered to other users and does not access any other person's Pinterest data.

What is stored, and where

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, encrypted

using Windows DPAPI under the owner's Windows user account. No Pinterest data, tokens,

or credentials are sent to or stored on any third-party server, and none are sold,

rented, or shared.

How data is used

Solely to publish the business's own content to its own Pinterest account on a schedule

set by the owner.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the App's access at any time in Pinterest account settings

(Apps / authorized apps). Revoking access immediately stops the App from working.
